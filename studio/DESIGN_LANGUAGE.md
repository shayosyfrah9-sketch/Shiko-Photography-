# Design language (v0, to be refined by concepts)
- Type: Latin display Jost 300 caps tracked .2–.35em; body Jost/Rubik 300–400; Hebrew Rubik/Heebo, never tracked. RTL for he, LTR for en.
- Colour: near-black #0b0a09, warm ivory #f3efe6, one accent per domain (festival amber/magenta, sport floodlight white-blue, portrait warm stone, video deep teal). Gold #f3dcb0 for booking.
- UI: hairlines (1px, 14–20% white), text buttons with thin underline, radius ≤ 3px, no heavy shadows, compact bottom dock.
- Photo frames: thin ivory mount ~1% of the short side, true aspect ratio, no crops.
- Light: golden hour by default; each domain has its own motivated light colour.
- Motion: cubic in-out, 0.9–1.6 s transitions, diegetic transitions (aperture, lens, portal), reduced-motion = cross-fades.
- Sound: silent until a gesture; ambience per area; one mute control.
