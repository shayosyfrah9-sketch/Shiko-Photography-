## Workflow for every significant output
1 research → 2 several original ideas → 3 pick the strongest → 4 first visuals → 5 render/screenshot → 6 independent critique (art-critic, scorecard in shiko-visual-critique) → 7 weaknesses → 8 improve → 9 side-by-side compare → 10 performance & UX check. Minimum 3 create/critique/improve rounds for key work. Judge images, never code.
