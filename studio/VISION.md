# Vision — SHIKO WORLD 2.0
"We are not building another photography website. We are building a whole world around the brand." (Shay, 2026-11-24)
- A cinematic, explorable 3D world that makes people stop and say WOW, and that clients forward to friends.
- The world is the stage; Shay's photographs are the art. Galleries, booking and WhatsApp are never more than one tap away.
- Four domains with iconic, independent architecture, recognisable by silhouette: FESTIVALS (organic, light, energy), SPORT (powerful stadium, floodlights, entrance tunnel), PORTRAITS & BRANDING (stone/glass/still-water museum), VIDEO (cinema with screens in the architecture).
- Quality references (not to copy): Unreal Engine 5 realism, Awwwards/FWA craft, Ghibli-level world atmosphere, international futurist architecture, high-end film, premium brands, modern museums.
- Avoid: generic, childish 3D, boxes with textures, plastic materials, flat light, effects without artistic reason.
- Concept candidates: A Floating World, B Cinematic Valley, C World Beyond the Lens (+ the studio's own ideas).
- Content that must survive: all 18 rooms, original photos, order, gallery links, contact details.
