## Shay's five criteria
### Originality: 7 / 10 (R1: 6)
- **Evidence.** The six-state sheet is the strongest image of the concept so far: the same frame becomes a festival, a stadium, a sea, a portrait set or pure bokeh, and the bokeh backplate under the photo is visibly "his world, out of focus". This is a real idea, not a skin.
- **Weaknesses.** The contents are literal icons of the fields (tents = festivals, stadium = sport), the same theme-park logic as the live alpine village. **Tilt-shift:** the sport state is a textbook fake-miniature frame (one sharp stripe between two blurred bands, high vantage). The blur on the pine and the tents is smooth and Gaussian-looking; only the point lights turn into discs, which is exactly the "fake blur" signature of tilt-shift apps.

### Visual quality: 5 / 10 (R1: 4)
- **Evidence.** The light is right for the minute; the bokeh discs from the string lights in the sport, surf and portrait states are beautiful and photographic; the viewer backplate now looks like a real f/1.2 background.
- **Weaknesses.** The two biggest objects in the frame look like game assets: **the mannequin crowd** (the first thing the eye lands on, because it is the brightest area) and **the glowing-box stadium**. The sea, which carries a whole field, is a flat grey plane with pencil lines. Composite artefacts (mask fringes, a cut-out stool) would be visible to any photographer.

### User experience: 6 / 10 (R1: 5)
- **Evidence.** The phone mock-up now uses the real vertical render; the in-world labels with leader lines point to the actual planes; the bottom bar (Works / Book a shoot / WhatsApp) is clear at 0 s; the viewer is unchanged and good.
- **Weaknesses.** The distance scale has only metres, so a non-photographer must map "700 m" to sport by themselves. The labels are too small and faint to read at 390 px. PORTRAIT points at an empty edge of the phone frame, because the set is not in the phone composition. The phone plate wastes its bottom ~30% on black ground and its top ~40% on empty sky. Only English LTR is shown; Hebrew RTL is the primary language. **Game-like:** floating labels with leader lines over a crowd of figures still reads like a strategy-game map.

### Brand fit: 5 / 10 (R1: 5)
- **Evidence.** The palette is now calm (indigo, apricot, muted canopy colours), with no colour takeover; the photo is untouched and the star in the viewer.
- **Weaknesses.** Shay's festivals are real people in water spray and dust. Faceless dolls with arms raised are the opposite of his work, and the frame reads as a computer game, which he ruled out by name. The palette tint toward the photo (12%) cannot be seen, so the "world takes its light from the photograph" idea is not yet delivered.

### Performance: 7 / 10 (R1: 7)
- **Evidence.** The six states come from one plate plus a layered blur composite, the method budgeted at about 1 MB on a phone with about 12 draw calls; the focus states need no extra world bytes.
- **Weaknesses.** Still no device measurement. The fringes around masts and the hill show that the layer masks need dilation and premultiplied blur, so more shader work. The phone first view (0.85–1.0 MB) still sits on the 1 MB line.

## Top 3 fixes for round 3
### 1. Replace the two game-asset tells: the crowd and the stadium
- **Crowd:** at dusk, render people as **backlit silhouettes** against the lit tents: body value ≤ 0.08 with a warm 2700 K rim from the string lights. Use varied clothing silhouettes (shorts, dresses, hats, a raised phone or water bottle), heights 1.55–1.95 m, and 60–100 figures in clusters, not a grid. Arms raised on ≤ 15% of them. No visors. No faces need to be visible, so no realistic face models are needed.
- **Stadium:** two tiers of raked seating (~30–35°), a roof cantilever over one long side, an open end so the **green pitch with white lines** is visible, floodlight pools on the grass, crowd speckle in the stands (1–2 px warm and cool dots). Delete the sand-coloured interior wall and make the front wall opaque. It should read as Bloomfield at 400–700 m, not a box.
- Squint test target: at 25% you should recognise "festival crowd" and "football ground" in 1 s each.

### 2. Give surf and portrait a real subject, and make the sea work
- **Surf:** 3–4 rows of **white water** (value ≥ 0.6, rows ≥ 3 px tall at 1600 px), 2–3 surfer silhouettes ≥ 8 px tall on the shoulder of a wave, and a glint path 40–80 px wide reflecting the apricot band. The surf-focus frame must have one thing sharp that is worth looking at.
- **Portrait set:** a 2.4 m backdrop at 1.5 m cannot fit a 50 mm frame (it would fill it). Move the set to **3–4 m in the right third**, linen at value 0.55–0.65, lit by one warm practical (a 3200 K lamp or softbox) so it is the warmest near element. Stool **250–320 px** tall, so it reads as near. Put the ground under the stool on the **same layer** as the stool (no sharp object on blurred ground). In the phone plate, keep the set inside x 520–740 of 780 px.
- **Bokeh kernel:** use a disc kernel (bright rim, cat's-eye toward the frame edges) on *every* layer, not only on point lights. Real disc blur on the pine and tents is the single best cure for the tilt-shift look in the sport state. Also cap near-layer blur in the sport state at ~40 px so the frame stops being a perfect sharp stripe.

### 3. Clean the composite, the phone frame and the labels
- **Masks:** dilate each layer's alpha 3–4 px and blur in premultiplied space; check the masts, the hill edge and the stool at 200% for fringes. Zero visible halos.
- **Phone plate:** re-pitch so the horizon sits at ~38% from the top and the black ground takes ≤ 15% of the height (now ~30%); the sky above the bough ≤ 25% (now ~40%).
- **Labels and scale:** field names on the scale (פורטרטים · פסטיבלים · ספורט · גלישה · וידאו) with metres as a small second line; label text ≥ 13 px at 390 px with ≥ 4.5:1 contrast (a 60% ink pill behind it); every label must point at something in frame. Show the Hebrew RTL version. Use the same calendar icon for booking on phone and desktop.
- **Tint:** 12% is invisible. Raise it to 20–25% on the bokeh discs only, and demonstrate it with two photos of opposite palettes (e.g. surf turquoise vs Maccabi yellow) side by side.
- Render the video "past ∞" frame.

## Verdict: **NOT YET**
Clear progress (5.1 → 5.7): the focus mechanism now works and is the best proof of originality in any concept so far, and the light is right. But the hero frame is dominated by mannequins and a box stadium, the surf field is an empty sea, and the sport state reads as tilt-shift. Shay asked for "not a computer game"; today the crowd alone would lose him. The six-state sheet can be shown to Shay **as a proof of mechanism only**, labelled "Rendered plate + layered composite, work in progress", not as visual quality.
