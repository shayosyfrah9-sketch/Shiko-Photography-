## Principle
Photos are the stars, the village is the stage. Every upgrade must answer:
1. Does it really improve visual quality?  2. Does it serve photos and brand?
3. Does it keep/improve UX?  4. Does the benefit justify complexity and performance cost?
No effects/assets/systems just because the tech allows.
